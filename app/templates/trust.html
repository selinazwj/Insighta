Trust at Insighta

How Insighta keeps responses real

Verification levels

Researchers choose one level per study.

Basic account check

Email code, Google or LinkedIn sign-in.

Account + professional check

Institutional email, LinkedIn, professional registry or participation history.

ID check via Didit

Identity documents are handled by Didit and never shown in Insighta's frontend.

Pre-screening

Researchers set the eligibility rules. Answer choices are randomized to reduce predictable patterns. AI can help word questions, but never decides who is eligible. Sensitive screening questions are blocked.

After submission

Built-in survey responses receive automatic quality checks. Unusual responses are flagged for researcher review. If a researcher enables the optional quality filter, below-threshold responses can be marked rejected for manual review.

AI drafts. Rules decide. People review.
