How Insighta works

Recruit participants, join studies, and keep track of progress in one place.

For researchers

1

Create your listing

Describe your study, audience, time commitment, and full participant reward. Add eligibility checks where needed.

2

Review, fund, and publish

See the total before paying by card or wallet. Niche audiences receive a manual quote before payment.

3

Track and review

Manage participation and review completions. After closeout and outstanding reviews, eligible unused cash returns to your chosen destination.

For participants

1

Find a study

Browse opportunities and check the eligibility, format, time, and reward before deciding to join.

2

Check eligibility and take part

Complete any required verification or screening, then follow the study instructions or book your session.

3

Track your progress

Use your dashboard to follow participation and reward reviews. Approved cash earnings can be withdrawn after Stripe setup.
