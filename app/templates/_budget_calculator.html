How pricing works

Your budget includes participant rewards and platform fees. Survey fees are 20% of the reward, with a $5 minimum per participant paid to Insighta. Participants receive their full reward separately.

Remote interviews: 25%, minimum $10/person. In-person studies: 25%, minimum $15/person. Targeted and highly niche audiences need a manual quote.
