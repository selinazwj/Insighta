Set your target audience

Filter by demographics, education, location, and more

Done ▾
This step is covered in the publish videos above — audience targeting is part of the publish flow.

On the publish page, scroll to Target Audience. Set filters like age range, education level, field of study, location, and language. Leave fields blank to allow all participants. The more specific your filters, the higher quality your responses.
