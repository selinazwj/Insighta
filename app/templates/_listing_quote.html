How pricing works

The platform fee is added to the full participant reward and paid to Insighta. Surveys: 20%, minimum $5/person. Remote interviews: 25%, minimum $10/person. In-person studies: 25%, minimum $15/person.

Raffle and volunteer listings still pay the platform minimum. Niche recruitment requires a manual quote.
