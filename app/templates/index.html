Why Insighta

Research belongs
to everyone.

We built Insighta because great research kept getting stuck — not for lack of ideas, but lack of access to real, diverse people.

Privacy by design
Profile information supports eligibility and matching. Participants review each listing and choose whether to enter its study flow.
Your time is valued
Every study shows time commitment and reward upfront. No surprises. Participants always know exactly what they're signing up for.
Real support
In-product feedback and support threads give researchers and participants a direct way to report issues and ask platform questions.
