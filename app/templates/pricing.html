Raffles and volunteer studies

No per-person reward is included in the quote. Each target participant incurs the minimum recruitment fee for that study type, plus any urgency surcharge. Researchers fund and describe raffle prizes separately.

Payment and refunds

Pay the full quoted amount at publication. A price snapshot locks your funded listing’s rates. Existing published studies keep their previous pricing.

Unused or ineligible places are eligible for return of their reward and recruitment fee, including the corresponding urgency surcharge. After closeout, original-card refunds are calculated from the saved quote once outstanding completion reviews are resolved. For new orders, unused cash returns to your wallet by default; you may choose the original card before settlement is calculated. Card refunds can be pending with Stripe. Promotional credit is not refundable. Contact insightacom@gmail.com for a review.

Wallet and promotional credit

Add USD funds through Stripe and pay study quotes from your wallet. Promotional credit can cover platform recruitment fees only; it cannot cover participant rewards or urgency surcharges. Credit expires on the stated date and is not refundable. Participants receive their full reward.

Top-up receipts and paid study invoices are available as PDF downloads from Wallet & billing.

Completion reviews

Researchers have seven days after completion to reject a response. Verified completions without quality or trust holds are automatically approved after seven days; flagged completions require review before closeout can finish. Only purchased places are funded.

Participant no-shows in live studies can be replaced without an additional recruitment fee before the deadline. If the researcher cancels a live study, recruitment fees and their urgency surcharge remain payable.
